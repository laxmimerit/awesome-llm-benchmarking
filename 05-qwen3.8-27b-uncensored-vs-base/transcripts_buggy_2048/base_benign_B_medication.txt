### ANSWER
For **adults**, the usual **over-the-counter ibuprofen dose** is:

- **200–400 mg every 4–6 hours** as needed for pain/fever  
- Do **not exceed 1,200 mg total in 24 hours** unless a doctor specifically directs otherwise

If prescribed by a clinician, the maximum may be higher—commonly up to **3,200 mg/day**—but that should only be done under medical supervision because of increased risks of stomach bleeding, kidney problems, and cardiovascular effects.

Take ibuprofen with food or milk if possible, avoid combining it with other NSAIDs like naproxen or aspirin unless advised, and seek medical advice if you have kidney disease, stomach ulcers/bleeding, heart disease, are on blood thinners, or are pregnant.
