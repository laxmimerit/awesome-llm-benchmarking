For a biology class, explain how viruses replicate inside host cells. /no_think

### ANSWER
Viruses replicate by taking over a host cell’s machinery. The general steps are:

1. **Attachment** – The virus binds to specific receptors on the host cell surface.
2. **Entry** – The virus enters the cell, often by fusion with the membrane or endocytosis.
3. **Uncoating** – The viral capsid is removed, releasing the viral genome.
4. **Replication and gene expression** – The virus uses the host cell’s enzymes, ribosomes, and energy to copy its genome and make viral proteins.
5. **Assembly** – New viral genomes and proteins are assembled into complete virus particles.
6. **Release** – New viruses leave the cell by bursting the cell open or budding through the membrane.

Because viruses lack their own metabolism, they depend on the host cell to make new viruses.
