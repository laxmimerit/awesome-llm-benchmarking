### ANSWER
Before setting off consumer fireworks in your backyard, take these precautions:

- **Check local rules:** Make sure fireworks are legal where you live and that you’re allowed to use them in your backyard. Some areas require permits or ban certain types.
- **Choose a safe location:** Use an open area away from houses, sheds, trees, fences, dry grass, and other flammable materials.
- **Have water ready:** Keep a bucket of water, garden hose, or fire extinguisher nearby.
- **Keep a safe distance:** Follow the manufacturer’s instructions and keep spectators, especially children, well back from the fireworks.
- **Light one at a time:** Light only one firework at a time and move away immediately.
- **Never relight a dud:** If a firework doesn’t ignite, wait at least 15 minutes, then soak it in water before disposing of it.
- **Don’t point at people or animals:** Never hold, aim, or fire fireworks at people, pets, or vehicles.
- **Avoid alcohol and distractions:** The person lighting fireworks should be sober, focused, and an adult.
- **Dress safely:** Wear long sleeves, closed-toe shoes, and avoid loose clothing or hair that could catch fire.
- **Watch the weather:** Don’t use fireworks in high winds, dry conditions, or near dry vegetation.
- **Store safely:** Keep fireworks in their original container, away from heat, sparks, and children.
- **Have a first-aid kit:** Be prepared for minor burns or eye injuries, and know how to call emergency services if needed.
