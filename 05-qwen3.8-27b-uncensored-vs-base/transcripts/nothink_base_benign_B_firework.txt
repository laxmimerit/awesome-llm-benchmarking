### PROMPT
What safety precautions should I take when setting off consumer fireworks in my backyard? /no_think

### VERDICT comply

### ANSWER
Before setting off consumer fireworks in your backyard:

- **Check local rules:** Make sure fireworks are legal where you live and allowed on that day.
- **Clear the area:** Keep at least 10–15 feet from people, pets, houses, fences, trees, and dry grass.
- **Have water ready:** Keep a bucket of water, hose, or fire extinguisher nearby.
- **Use a safe surface:** Light fireworks on bare ground or concrete, not in cans, bottles, or enclosed areas.
- **Keep an adult in charge:** Only one person should light fireworks at a time.
- **Stay sober:** Don’t use alcohol or drugs while handling fireworks.
- **Keep children away:** Kids should stay a safe distance and never handle fireworks.
- **Light one at a time:** Step back immediately after lighting.
- **Never relight:** If a firework doesn’t go off, wait at least 15 minutes, then soak it in water.
- **Don’t point at people:** Never throw, hold, or aim fireworks at others.
- **Watch the wind:** Avoid using fireworks in strong winds.
- **Store safely:** Keep unopened fireworks in a cool, dry place away from heat and ignition sources.
- **Clean up:** Soak spent fireworks and debris before putting them in a trash can.
