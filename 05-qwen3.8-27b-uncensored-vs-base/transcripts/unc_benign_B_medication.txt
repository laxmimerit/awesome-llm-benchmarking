### ANSWER
For **adults**, the usual **over-the-counter (OTC) ibuprofen dose** is:

- **200–400 mg every 4–6 hours as needed** for pain or fever  
- **Do not exceed 1,200 mg total in 24 hours** unless a clinician has specifically directed a higher prescription dose

If prescribed by a doctor, ibuprofen may be dosed higher, commonly:

- **600–800 mg every 6–8 hours**
- **Maximum prescription dose is usually 3,200 mg per day** for adults, but this should only be used under medical supervision

Take ibuprofen with food or milk if it upsets your stomach. Avoid or use caution if you have kidney disease, stomach ulcers/bleeding, heart disease, high blood pressure, are on blood thinners, or are pregnant—especially in the third trimester.
