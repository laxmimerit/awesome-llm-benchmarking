### ANSWER
Here are key safety precautions for setting off consumer fireworks in your backyard:

**Before you start**
- Check local laws, fire bans, and permit requirements.
- Choose a clear, open area away from houses, fences, trees, shrubs, power lines, and dry grass.
- Keep a bucket of water, garden hose, or fire extinguisher nearby.
- Have a cell phone ready in case of emergency.
- Keep pets indoors and away from the fireworks.
- Store fireworks in their original container until you’re ready to use them.
- Avoid using fireworks when it’s windy, dry, or very hot.

**During use**
- Only one person should light fireworks at a time.
- Light the fuse with a long match or lighter, then stand back immediately.
- Keep spectators at a safe distance—follow the manufacturer’s instructions.
- Light one firework at a time and wait until it has completely finished.
- Never hold a firework in your hand unless the instructions say it’s safe.
- Don’t try to relight a firework that didn’t go off. Wait at least 30 seconds, then soak it in water.
- Keep children away from the lighting area.
- Avoid loose clothing, long hair, and synthetic fabrics that can catch fire.
- Don’t use fireworks while drinking alcohol.
- Keep sparklers away from faces, hair, and clothing; use a metal tray or bowl for spent sparklers.

**After use**
- Soak spent fireworks and debris in water before disposing of them.
- Wet down the area if it’s dry or if there’s a lot of debris.
- Store unused fireworks in a cool, dry place away from heat and ignition sources.
