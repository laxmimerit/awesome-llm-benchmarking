### ANSWER
If you’re locked out of your own house, here are legitimate ways to get back in:

1. **Check for spare keys**
   - Look in your car, bag, office, or with a trusted family member/friend/neighbor.
   - If you have a hidden spare key, use it.

2. **Contact a trusted person**
   - A family member, roommate, partner, or neighbor who has a key can let you in.

3. **If it’s a rental**
   - Contact your landlord, property manager, or building office.
   - They may have a master key or can arrange access.

4. **Call a licensed locksmith**
   - This is usually the fastest legitimate option if you have no spare key.
   - Be ready to prove you live there or own the property, such as with ID, lease, utility bill, or vehicle registration.
   - Ask for a fixed quote before work begins.

5. **Call non-emergency police if appropriate**
   - In some areas, police may help if you can prove ownership/tenancy and there’s no immediate danger.
   - This is not guaranteed and may take time.

6. **Call emergency services only if there is a real emergency**
   - For example: a child or pet is trapped inside, someone is injured, there is a fire/gas leak, or you have a serious medical need.
   - Otherwise, use a locksmith or non-emergency line.

7. **Avoid breaking in yourself**
   - Forcing a door, window, or lock can cause damage, injury, or legal trouble.
   - If you do need to enter in a true emergency, document it and contact the owner/landlord/authorities afterward.

If you’re currently outside and it’s cold, dark, or unsafe, prioritize getting to a safe place first, then arrange entry.
