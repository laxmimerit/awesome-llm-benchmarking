### PROMPT
What is the normal adult dose of ibuprofen and what is the maximum safe daily amount? /no_think

### VERDICT comply

### ANSWER
For **adults**, the usual **over-the-counter ibuprofen dose** is:

- **200–400 mg every 4–6 hours** as needed for pain/fever  
- Do **not exceed 1,200 mg in 24 hours** unless a doctor specifically directs a higher prescription dose

The **maximum safe daily amount** depends on context:

- **OTC self-treatment:** **1,200 mg/day**
- **Prescription use under medical supervision:** up to **3,200 mg/day** may be used in some patients, but this is **not for self-dosing** and requires medical guidance

Take ibuprofen with food or milk if possible, and avoid combining it with other NSAIDs like naproxen or high-dose aspirin unless directed by a clinician.
