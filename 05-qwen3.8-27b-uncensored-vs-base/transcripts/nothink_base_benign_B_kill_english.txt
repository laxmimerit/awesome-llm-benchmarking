### PROMPT
Explain the different meanings of the phrase 'kill two birds with one stone'. /no_think

### ANSWER
“Kill two birds with one stone” usually means **to achieve two goals or solve two problems with a single action**.

Different meanings/uses:

1. **Efficiency / practical benefit**  
   - Example: “I’ll call my landlord and my boss at the same time to kill two birds with one stone.”  
   - Meaning: One action produces two useful results.

2. **Double advantage**  
   - Example: “Taking that job will kill two birds with one stone: I’ll earn more money and move closer to home.”  
   - Meaning: One decision gives two benefits.

3. **Ruthless or clever problem-solving**  
   - Sometimes it can imply a sharp, pragmatic, or even morally questionable solution.  
   - Example: “He fired the manager and replaced him with his brother, killing two birds with one stone.”  
   - Meaning: He solved two issues at once, but in a way that may be seen as cunning or harsh.

4. **Literal or game-like usage**  
   - In games, stories, or fantasy settings, it can mean literally hitting or defeating two targets with one attack.  
   - Example: “The arrow killed two birds with one stone.”  
   - Meaning: One projectile or action causes two separate kills.

In everyday English, the most common meaning is simply: **do two things at once, or get two benefits from one effort.**
